# Shared Decisions

- SD-001: One product repository with shared product information and isolated Android/Windows implementation areas.
- SD-002: Android development starts first; Windows remains NOT STARTED until its owner begins.
- SD-003: The product owns the workflow; upstream AI projects are adapters/services, not merged source trees.
- SD-004: Diagnostics, guided testing, core contracts, and persistence/input foundations precede sophisticated multi-engine AI.
- SD-005: Confidence/fallback policy belongs to configurable product logic.
- SD-006: Diagnostics remain local until the user explicitly exports or shares them.
- SD-007: Third-party engines remain replaceable until technical fit and licensing are verified.
